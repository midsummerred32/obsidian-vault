## Carbohydrates 
- Monosaccharides are 1 sugar
	- Typically CH<sub>2</sub>O (1:2:1 Ratio)
- Polysaccharides are the polymer form
- There are storage polysaccharides to store energy.
	- Starch is an example.
	- Glycogen is another.
- Structural Polysaccharides are building materials.
	- Cellulose is in cell wall.
		- Insoluble Fiber
	- Different glycolic linkages make different functions.
	- Chitin used by arthropods to make exoskeletons.

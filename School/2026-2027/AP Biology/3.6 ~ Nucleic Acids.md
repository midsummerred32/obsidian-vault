## Nucleic Acids
- The amino acid sequence of a polypeptide programmed by a nucleic acid is called a gene.
- DNA and RNA allow living organisms to reproduce.
- DNA can direct synthesis of RNA.
- DNA --> RNA --> Protein
- DNA stays in nucleus. 
## Components
- Polymer is polynucleotide.
- Nucleotide consists of:
	- nitrogenous base
	- five carbon sugar (a pentose)
	- one to three phosphate groups
- 2 types of nitrogenous bases
	- pyrimidine has one six membered ring of carbon and nitrogen.
		- Cytosine 
		- Thymine
		- Uracil
	- Purines is a six membered ring fused to a five-membered ring.
		- Adenine
		- Guanine
- Attached to sugar
	- In DNA, Deoxyribose
	- In RNA, Ribose
- Deoxyribose lacks an oxygen.
## Nucleotide Polymers
- Polymers involve dehydration reaction.
- Repeating units of sugar phosphate backbone
- Order of bases changes function
## Structure
- DNA molecules have 2 polynucleotides that form double helix
	- Also known as antiparrallel
- G goes with C, A with T
- RNA is one strand
- T replaced with U in RNA

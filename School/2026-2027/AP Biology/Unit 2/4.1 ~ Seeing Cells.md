## Mircoscopy
- Robert Hooke used a microscope to see cell walls first.
- Light mircroscopes are most commonly used.
- 3 important parameters
	- Magnification
	- Resolution
	- Contrast
- Electron microscope used to see organelles.
- Transmission electron microscope is used to study the internal structure of cells.
- Electron microscopes kill cells.
- A useful technique for cell structure and function is cell fractionation.

## Mosaic
- Cell membranes are made from phospholipids.
- The heads are hydrophillic, while the tails are hydrophobic.
- Some proteins are attached/go through the membrane.
- Cholesterol sometimes inside.

## Fluidity
- Lipids and some proteins can move side to side.
- Phospholipids can flip sides but not very often.
- Saturated tails pack together and creates viscosity.
## Transmembrane Protein
- Ends that push into cytoplasm and extracelluar.
- Jobs
	- Transport
	- Enzymatic activity
	- Signal transduction
	- Cell-cell recognition
	- Intercelluar joining
	- Attachment to cytoskeleton and extracelluar matrix

## Functional Groups
### Hydrocarbon
- All hydrogen and carbon
- Lipids
### Hydroxyl Group
- OH (or HO)
- Creates an alcohol
- Ethane --> add OH --> Ethanol
### Carbonyl Group
- C double bonded to O
- Polar
- Creates sugars
### Carboxyl Group
- C double bonded to O and C bonded to O to H
- Polar
- Creates organic acid
### Amino Group
- N bonded to 2 Hs
- Creates bases
